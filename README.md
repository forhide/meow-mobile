# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.58.0** — evolved scepters awaken their special (new name, stronger, and every foe hit gets steam, frost, fire, roots, rain or sparks), wear their own colour and sparkle, and Pip tells you when a wand is ready to evolve (with a one-press 'Wear' for the right piece). Every wand card shows its evolution path.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.58.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
