# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.46.0** — every chapter boss now fights its own way: the Giant Broom throws dust and dashes off walls, the Overdue Stamp slams and leaves ink, the topiary grows hedge walls and throws boomerang leaves, the Giant Gull dives from the sky. Bosses have a big health bar at the top and can be hit anywhere on their body. New harbour foes (crab, jellyfish, bubble slime) and potted topiaries, props that fit each place, hopping mushrooms, and a steaming teacup that shows where to save.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.46.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
