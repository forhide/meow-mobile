# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.63.0** — Pip's specials are now staged: the chicken lands and pecks before an elemental pop, the eagle sends three whirlwinds from behind the cat, and the elemental dragon crosses the screen three times breathing water, ice and lightning. Bosses absorb their mana as they grow and get proper defeat and recovery scenes; the stage tracker and minimap are gone; Pip's wand growth now always asks before eating a wand.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.63.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Local browser music (7/7) and isolated sound-effect checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio, hardware and offline update testing were not done for this build. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
