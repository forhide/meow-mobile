# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.66.0** — Pip's summon scene now matches what you have earned when you enter Pip's shop (eagle or dragon, each once per run). The topiary copy-cat is born from a laser scan and real garden shears snipping the shrub. The dragon special flies at half speed above the cat, and its breath is a soft pastel wash. Everything from v0.65.0 is included.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.66.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Local browser music (7/7) and isolated sound-effect checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio, hardware and offline update testing were not done for this build. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
