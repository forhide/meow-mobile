# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.65.0** — Phone controls retuned after playtesting: the move stick keeps its smaller size but sits a little further from the corner, and the spell, special and roll buttons are a bit larger and also a little further in. Everything from v0.63.0 (staged Pip specials, boss awakening and defeat scenes) is included.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.65.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Local browser music (7/7) and isolated sound-effect checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio, hardware and offline update testing were not done for this build. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
