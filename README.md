# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.55.0** — better wands now do more, not just look bigger (more fan shards and bounces, wider bursts, longer boomerangs, stronger waves); the water wave stays readable, the wind boomerang spins, and a stunned boss wobbles under dizzy stars. Pip's paired wand fusion and the six spell rules from v0.54.0 are included.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.55.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
