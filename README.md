# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.44.3** — two new recorded themes (the Folio Warden midboss and the Ordered Promenade), the binding relay shows a segmented health gauge, one bottom popup moves you on at the gate (you now stand on the gate itself; fight seals answer at their edge), no repeated passage captions, plus everything in v0.44.2: caught miscasts turn into homing overreactions, the phone action popup, softer casting strain and stronger chicken and eagle specials.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.44.3** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
