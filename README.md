# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.44.6** — on phones a tapped landing special (meteors, groves, metal blooms, chain lightning) drops on the biggest group of foes in reach, and the fight seal has a cream centre that shows on every floor; plus v0.44.5: English buttons stay on one line, see-through cat remarks, story rune ribbons and a bigger dragon.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.44.6** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
