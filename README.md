# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.48.0** — painted boss fights: Big자루's dust, 꾹장님's stamping and ink, 복붙냥's continuous vines, 걸리버's gust and dive, and a redesigned Grimoire freed from its new sealed tower in the finale. Plus v0.47.3: bolder Dancing Lightning and a fire-and-water Pocket steam.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.48.0** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
