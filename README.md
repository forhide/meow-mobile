# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — web / phone build

Play: https://forhide.github.io/meow-mobile/

Current build: **v0.44.8** — the Grimoire final fight now really attacks: its announced page beam is no longer cancelled by hits and is drawn as a lavender ray, the warning is a rounded rune stroke, and from afar it throws a fan of flying books; it also has more HP. Plus v0.44.7: on PC landing specials fall at the cursor.

On iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn the phone sideways. The app starts at index.html and supports offline play after a complete first load.

For updates, connect to the Internet, close every game window/tab, and reopen. Check **v0.44.8** at the bottom left of the title. Keep browser/app storage to retain progress; deleting it is unnecessary. Chromium update/offline and Web Audio checks passed. Actual iPhone/Safari audio and hardware testing remain separate checks. Sound preferences are preserved; Settings → Check sound can help verify output.
