Every ZIP is in two pieces because GitHub limits one file to 100 MB.
Download both pieces of your platform into the same folder, then join them.

  Windows (Command Prompt):
    copy /b Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-windows.zip.001+Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-windows.zip.002 Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-windows.zip
  macOS, Apple Silicon (Terminal):
    cat Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-macos.zip.001 Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-macos.zip.002 > Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-macos.zip
  Linux:
    cat Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-linux.zip.001 Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-linux.zip.002 > Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-linux.zip
  Web (static hosting), same way:
    cat Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-web.zip.001 Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-web.zip.002 > Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-web.zip

The joined ZIP's SHA-256 is listed in Meowgnificent-v0.43.1-SHA256SUMS.txt
(Windows: certutil -hashfile <zip> SHA256 · macOS: shasum -a 256 <zip>).
